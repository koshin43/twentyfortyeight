# 2048 — Design Spec

Date: 2026-10-01
Status: Draft

## 1. Purpose

A personal 2048: the classic 4×4 sliding-tile game with no ads, popups or tracking, plus what other versions lack — stats, playing on past the win, and a 3-minute **Blitz** pace.

The owner plays equally on a phone (iPhone and Android) and a laptop. Each device keeps its own games and stats.

Success looks like:

- The app opens instantly, including offline, straight onto the board.
- Swipes feel immediate, with tiles sliding smoothly like the original.

## 2. Decisions already made

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| App type | Installable web app (PWA) named "2048". Phone and laptop are equally important. |
| Data location | On the device only. No accounts, no server, no sync. Each device has its own game and stats. |
| Board | 4×4 only. Win tile 2048. Play always continues past it. |
| Rules | Classic 2048 only. No variant tiles, no powers, no undo. |
| Pace | **Endless** or **Blitz** (3-minute timed game). One game at a time; changing pace starts a new game. Each pace has its own stats: 2 stat sets. |
| Scoring | Each merge adds the new tile's value. No multipliers. |
| Stats | Per pace: best score, best tile, games played, wins and win rate, top 10. No reset. |
| Look | The original 2048 palette, light only. |
| Feedback | Generated sound effects and vibration (where the device allows it), one on/off toggle. |
| Hosting | GitHub Pages, deployed by a workflow on push to `master`. |

## 3. Rules

- A new game places two tiles in random empty cells. Each is a 2 (90%) or a 4 (10%).
- A swipe (up, down, left, right) slides every tile as far as it can go in that direction. Two equal tiles that meet merge into one tile of double the value.
- Each tile merges at most once per swipe. Merges resolve starting from the edge the swipe moves towards. Swiping left: `2 2 2 _` → `4 2 _ _`; `2 2 2 2` → `4 4 _ _`; `4 4 8 _` → `8 8 _ _`.
- A swipe that changes nothing is ignored: no tile appears and it does not count as a move.
- A **move** is a swipe that changes the board. After every move one new tile appears in a random empty cell: a 2 (90%) or a 4 (10%).
- Each merge adds the resulting tile's value to the score.
- **Best tile** is the highest tile the game has ever had.
- A game is **won** once its best tile reaches 2048. A won game stays won.
- **Game over**: no swipe would change the board.
- **Blitz** also ends when its clock reaches 0:00 ("Time's Up").

## 4. Game flow

### 4.1 Opening the app

- The app opens straight onto the board and resumes the saved game exactly as it was left.
- With no saved game (first launch), it starts an Endless game.
- If saved data fails validation, see 9.2.

### 4.2 Starting a new game

- **New Game** opens the New Game panel (6.2), with the current game's pace selected.
- **Start** ends the current game and begins a new one with the chosen pace.
- Closing the panel (✕, tapping outside it, or Esc) keeps the current game.
- Changing pace happens only through this panel.

### 4.3 Blitz clock

- The clock counts down from 3:00 and is shown in the TIME box.
- It starts on the first swipe of the game.
- It pauses whenever the app is hidden (switching apps, locking the phone) and whenever the New Game panel or the Stats screen is open. While paused after starting, the TIME box label reads "PAUSED".
- A paused clock resumes on the next swipe.
- The clock keeps running during the win banner.
- The saved game always holds the time left; a reopened Blitz game is paused until the next swipe.

### 4.4 Win

- **Endless:** the first time the game is won, a win overlay covers the board: "You made 2048!", with **Keep Going** and **New Game**. Keep Going closes it for the rest of the game; New Game opens the New Game panel.
- **Blitz:** a banner "You made 2048!" shows over the top of the board for about 2 seconds; play is never paused.
- When one move both wins and ends the game, only the end overlay shows.

### 4.5 Game end

- An end overlay covers the board: "Game Over" or "Time's Up", the final score, the best tile, "New best!" when the score beats the pace's previous best score (or the pace has no games yet), and **New Game**, which opens the New Game panel.
- A finished game stays on screen with its end overlay, including after reopening the app, until a new game starts.

### 4.6 Recording games

- A game is recorded in its pace's stats when it ends (game over or time's up), or when it is abandoned by starting a new game while it is unfinished and its score is above 0.
- A game is recorded exactly once. Starting a new game after a finished game records nothing more.
- A record holds the final score, the best tile and the date it ended. Whether it was won follows from its best tile.
- The game screen's BEST box shows the larger of the pace's best recorded score and the current score.

## 6. Screens

There is one screen, the game screen, with two dialogs over it: the New Game panel and the Stats screen. On a laptop the same single column is centred.

### 6.1 Game screen

Top to bottom:

1. "2048" title, then SCORE and BEST boxes.
2. The pace label ("Endless" or "Blitz"), and in Blitz the TIME box.
3. **New Game** button, Stats button (📊), sound toggle (🔊 / 🔇).
4. The board: square, as large as the screen allows. Win and end overlays cover the board only; the Blitz win banner sits over its top edge.

A notice banner (9.2) appears at the top when saved data was replaced; it closes with ✕.

### 6.2 New Game panel

A panel rising from the bottom of the screen:

- Title "New Game", close ✕.
- **Pace:** Endless | Blitz, a segmented control.
- When the current game is unfinished, a warning line: "Your current game ends here." If it has points, it continues: "Its 1,284 points will be recorded." A finished game shows no warning.
- **Start**.

### 6.3 Stats screen

A full-screen dialog over the game, opened by the Stats button, closed by ✕ or Esc.

- Title "Stats", close ✕.
- Two cells side by side, Endless and Blitz. Each shows its pace's name, best score and best tile, or "—" with no games. Tapping a cell selects it.
- Below the cells, for the selected pace: its name, games played, wins with win rate ("9 · 24%"), and **Top 10**: rank, score, ✓ if won, best tile (as a small tile-colored chip), date. With no games: "No games yet".
- The screen opens with the current game's pace selected.

## 7. Input, motion and feedback

### 7.1 Input

- **Laptop:** arrow keys or W/A/S/D swipe. Mouse clicks press buttons. Esc closes the open dialog.
- **Phone:** a swipe on the board moves in the direction of its larger axis, after a short minimum distance. Taps press buttons. The page never scrolls, zooms or pull-to-refreshes while playing.
- A swipe made while tiles are still animating is accepted immediately; the running animation jumps to its end.
- Swipes are ignored while an overlay or dialog covers the board.

### 7.2 Motion

- Tiles slide to their new cells in about 100ms. Merged tiles pop (a brief scale up and back). New tiles grow in.
- With reduced motion requested by the device, tiles jump to their new cells and nothing pops or grows.

### 7.3 Sound and vibration

Sounds are generated in the browser:

| Event | Sound | Vibration |
|---|---|---|
| Move | Soft tick | — |
| Merge | Pop, higher pitch for higher tile values | — |
| New best tile in this game (from 128) | Bright chime | Short |
| Win | Fanfare | Long |
| Game Over / Time's Up | Falling tone | Long |

- One toggle (🔊 / 🔇) turns sound and vibration on and off together. It starts on and is remembered on the device.
- Vibration happens only where the device supports it (not on iPhone). On iPhone, sound starts after the first touch, as the browser requires.

## 8. Data model

Stored as one JSON value under the `localStorage` key `2048`.

```ts
type Pace = 'endless' | 'blitz';

interface Save {
  sound: boolean;    // sound and vibration on
  game: Game;        // the current game; always present after first launch
  stats: Stats;
}

interface Tile {
  id: number;
  row: number;
  col: number;
  value: number;
}

interface Game {
  tiles: Tile[];
  nextTileId: number;
  score: number;
  random: number;          // seeded generator state, unsigned 32-bit
  pace: Pace;
  bestTile: number;
  winSeen: boolean;        // Endless win overlay answered with Keep Going
  blitzMsLeft: number | null; // present exactly when pace is 'blitz'; 0..180000
}

interface GameRecord {
  score: number;
  bestTile: number;
  endedAt: number;         // epoch milliseconds
}

interface SetStats {
  played: number;
  wins: number;
  bestTile: number;        // 0 when played is 0
  top: GameRecord[];       // at most 10; highest score first, ties earliest first
}

type Stats = Record<Pace, SetStats>;  // both keys always present
```

Rules:

- Every field is always present. Optional values are `null`, never omitted.
- Derived, never stored: whether a game or record is won (best tile ≥ 2048), whether a game has ended (3), a pace's best score (`top[0].score`), and the pace the New Game panel starts on (the current game's).
- Tiles lie within the 4×4 board, never share a cell, and have unique ids below `nextTileId`. Values are powers of two, at least 2.
- Score and best tile are non-negative integers. Best tile is a power of two at least as large as every tile on the board.
- `winSeen` is `false` in Blitz games and in games not yet won.
- In stats, `wins` ≤ `played`, `top.length` ≤ min(`played`, 10), `wins` is at least the number of won records in `top`, and `bestTile` is at least every record's best tile.
- A new game's random seed comes from `crypto.getRandomValues`. The rules draw every random number from the game's own generator state.

## 9. Storage and offline

### 9.1 Saving

- The whole `Save` is written in one `localStorage` write, so a game and its stats can never disagree.
- It is written after every change: each move, Keep Going, new game and sound toggle. It is also written when the app is hidden, which keeps Blitz time left.

### 9.2 Reading and validation

- On launch the save is parsed and each part (`sound`, `game`, `stats`) is strictly validated on its own against section 8. Unknown fields, missing fields and broken rules are failures.
- A failing part is replaced, never repaired: `game` by a new Endless game; `stats` by empty stats; `sound` by on. Unparseable JSON, or a top-level field other than these three, replaces everything.
- When anything was replaced, a notice banner says "Some saved data couldn't be read and was reset:" followed by what (for example "your game, your stats").
- There is no version field and no migration. Any future change to the saved shape makes older saves fail validation and be replaced. This is accepted.

### 9.3 Offline and updates

- A service worker caches the app, so it opens and plays fully offline after the first visit.
- New versions install automatically when online. If an update reloads the page mid-game, the game resumes exactly where it was, with Blitz paused.
- Known risk, accepted: clearing the browser's site data, or losing the device, loses the game and stats.

## 10. Tech stack

- React + TypeScript, built with Vite. CSS Modules beside components; theme values as CSS custom properties defined once.
- No UI component library. Dialogs (New Game panel, Stats screen) are native `<dialog>` elements. The pace choice is styled radio inputs.
- No router: the Stats screen is a dialog over the game.
- `vite-plugin-pwa` for the manifest, icons and service worker.
- Browser platform for everything else: Pointer Events and keyboard events for input, CSS transitions for motion, Web Audio for sounds, the Vibration API, `crypto.getRandomValues` for seeds, `localStorage` for the save.
- A small seeded 32-bit generator (mulberry32) written in the game rules.
- Hosting: GitHub Pages via a workflow on push to `master` that installs, tests, lints, builds and deploys.

## 11. Testing

There are two integration suites and no other tests (AGENTS.md, Testing). Each item below is an edge case that a plausible implementation gets wrong. If the code breaks one of them, at least one test must fail.

### 11.1 Rules scenarios (`game/`)

Each scenario starts from a board written as text and a fixed generator state. It calls the public `game/` API and compares the whole resulting board, score and events.

- **Slide geometry.** One asymmetric board, swiped in all four directions, gives four expected boards and scores. This catches row/column mix-ups and reversed lines.
- **Line table.** Each row below is the only filled row of the board. Each case also checks the score from its merges.

  | Row | Left | Right |
  |---|---|---|
  | `2 2 2 2` | `4 4 _ _` | `_ _ 4 4` |
  | `2 2 2 _` | `4 2 _ _` | `_ _ 2 4` |
  | `4 4 8 _` | `8 8 _ _` | `_ _ 8 8` |
  | `2 _ _ 2` | `4 _ _ _` | `_ _ _ 4` |
  | `2 4 _ _` | ignored | `_ _ 2 4` |

- **Spawned 4.** A move whose new tile is a 4 above the previous best tile raises the best tile, and the game stays valid.
- **Game end.**
  - A full board with one merge left isn't over; with none, it is.
  - A move that both wins and ends the game reports both.
- **Validation table.** Each row takes one valid Blitz game, changes one thing, and expects it to be rejected. The unchanged game is accepted. Rows:
  - a tile out of bounds; two tiles in one cell
  - a duplicate id; an id ≥ nextTileId
  - a value of 3
  - bestTile below a tile
  - winSeen in Blitz
  - blitzMsLeft above 180,000
  - an unknown field

### 11.2 App flows (`app/`)

The whole app renders with the real game, save and stats code over jsdom's `localStorage`. Each flow starts from a crafted save, or none. It plays through keys, pointer drags and clicks, then checks what the player sees and what is saved. Only these are stubbed: the sound and vibration module, timers, `crypto.getRandomValues`, and page visibility.

1. **Resume exactly.**
   - First launch shows an Endless game with two tiles and no notice.
   - Arrow-key moves and a pointer drag work; a drag shorter than the minimum distance does nothing.
   - After a remount, the board, score and BEST are exactly the same.
   - Sound toggled off stays off after the remount and plays nothing.
2. **Corrupt save** (table).
   - Sound, game and stats are each corrupted alone; game and stats through a broken invariant, not just a wrong type. Only that part resets, and the notice names it.
   - Unparseable JSON, and a save with an unknown top-level field, reset everything.
3. **Recorded exactly once.**
   - Start from a crafted game one move from game over, in a pace whose 10th-best score equals the final score.
   - The move shows Game Over without "New best!". Stats shows played + 1 and leaves the new game out of the top 10, because ties keep the earlier game.
   - Remounting shows the finished game and records nothing. New Game then records nothing more.
   - The same ending in an empty pace shows "New best!".
4. **New Game panel.**
   - It opens with the current game's pace selected, and arrow keys behind it don't move the board.
   - Esc and ✕ leave the current game untouched.
   - Abandoning a game with points shows the points line and records the game in its own pace. A game with 0 points shows no points line and records nothing.
   - The started pace shows in the pace label and is selected the next time the panel opens.
5. **Blitz clock** (fake timers).
   - The clock stays at 3:00 until the first swipe.
   - It stops and shows PAUSED while Stats is open and while the page is hidden, and resumes on the next swipe.
   - A remount comes back paused at the same time.
   - The win banner doesn't stop it.
   - At 0:00 it shows Time's Up and records the game.
6. **Stats screen.**
   - From a crafted save, each pace's cell shows its best score and best tile, or "—" with no games.
   - It opens on the current game's pace.
   - Selecting a cell shows that pace's played, wins · rate and Top 10, with ✓ on won games. An empty pace shows "No games yet".

### 11.3 Manual

Install on an Android phone, an iPhone and a laptop browser. Check:

- swipe feel and animation smoothness
- sounds, and vibration on Android
- offline launch
- resume after closing the app mid-Blitz

## 12. Out of scope for v1

- Undo; hints, solvers or AI.
- Accounts, sync, backup or export, sharing, leaderboards.
- Dark mode, themes, other palettes.
- Board sizes other than 4×4; variant tiles, powers or other rule styles; Blitz durations other than 3 minutes.
- Daily boards, golden tiles, combo or streak scoring.
- Stats reset; full game history beyond the top 10.
- Swipe-to-close on the New Game panel.
- Settings beyond the sound toggle.
